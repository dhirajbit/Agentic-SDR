Questions

The skeptical operator's FAQ

Does it really replace my inbound SDR team?+
It takes over the grind: finding the right leads, researching them, reaching out, following up until people answer, and booking the meeting. Your closers spend their time in meetings it books, not chasing cold lists. Most teams stop hiring for the top of the funnel.
Where do the leads come from?+
Two ways. It can work intent signals from your website — people showing they're in-market — or prospect straight from the records already in your CRM. Either way, no list-buying required.
Do I have to build sequences or templates?+
No. Point it at your site and your CRM and it works out who buys from you and recommends the angle, the channel order and the timing per lead. You approve the strategy; it runs it.
Why is WhatsApp such a big deal?+
In India, cold email mostly gets ignored and WhatsApp gets answered. A human-style WhatsApp follow-up riding on top of email is the single biggest lift in reply rate we see. It's paced and warmed up like a person, used as a follow-up to people you've already emailed, never cold spray.
Does it use my own domain, number and tools?+
Yes. It runs inside your stack — your CRM, your email sender, your WhatsApp number, your calendar. It's your prospector, not a third-party blaster. Opt-outs are permanent and your data stays yours.
